ABOUT THE GALLERY

Every glance upward tells a different story.

The sky is an ever-changing canvas painted with light, color, and emotion. Every sunrise promises a new beginning, every cloud tells its own story, every ray of sunlight brings warmth, and every sunset leaves behind memories worth holding onto.

This gallery is thoughtfully organized into four collections — Clouds, Color Sky, Sun, and Night Clicks — making it easy to explore each perspective of the sky through my lens.
